\subsection*{Overview}

This 24 hours course aims at acquainting students with relevant microeconomics methods to tackle finance issues.

\begin{enumerate}

\item Decision Making under Uncertainty
\item Equilibrium in Markets for Securities
\item Investment Decision under Market Imperfections: the Principal-Agent Problem
\end{enumerate}
